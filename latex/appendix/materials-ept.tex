\chapter{Elicited Production Task Materials}\label{app-materials-ept}

\widowpenalties 1 10000
\raggedbottom

An oral elicited production task (EPT) was included in both the direct object relative clause (ORC) study and the subject relative clause (SRC) study. Participants comprised English native speakers (ENSs), L1-Korean L2ers of English (KLEs), and L1-Mandarin L2ers of English (MLEs). Warning messages were presented to participants in their native language. Test = test sentence; Foil = foil sentence; Target = target response.
