\chapter{Self-Paced Reading Task Materials}\label{app-materials-spr}

A self-paced reading task (SPRT) was included in both the direct object relative clause (ORC) study and the subject relative clause (SRC) study. Participants comprised English native speakers (ENSs), L1-Korean L2ers of English (KLEs), and L1-Mandarin L2ers of English (MLEs). Warning messages were presented to participants in their native language. S = stimulus sentence; Q = comprehension question response options with correct answers underlined.

%========================================================%
\section{Warning messages in the ORC and SRC self-paced reading tasks}
%========================================================%

\excnt = 1

\ex % 1
Incorrect Answer\par\nobreak
Incorrect. Please read more carefully.\par\nobreak
\ko 오답입니다. 더욱 주의깊게 읽어주시기 바랍니다.\normalfont\par\nobreak
\zh 不正確。 請仔細閱讀。\normalfont
\xe

\ex % 2
Reading Too Fast\par\nobreak
You're going too fast. Please read the sentences more carefully.\par\nobreak
\ko 속도가 지나치게 빠릅니다. 문장을 더욱 주의 깊게 읽어주시기 바랍니다.\normalfont\par\nobreak
\zh 您回答得太快了。 請更仔細地閱讀句子。\normalfont
\xe
